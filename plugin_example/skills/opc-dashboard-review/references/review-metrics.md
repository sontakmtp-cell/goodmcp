# Operating metric definitions
Agree period, timezone, source and event definitions first. Separate unique people
from events. Count each eligible person once per person-based funnel stage;
deduplicate only where identity is known.

| Metric | Definition | Interpretation check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligible reach | Unique target people actually reached | Not planned recipients or impressions |
| Lead capture rate | Unique captured leads / unique eligible people reached | Same cohort, channel and period |
| Purchase conversion | Unique first buyers / unique eligible leads offered the product | Define exposure and allow time to purchase |
| Offer response rate | Unique responders / unique eligible offer recipients | Replies differ from paid demand |
| Cash received | Actual receipts minus refunds in period | Separate from bookings, invoiced revenue and profit |
| Unit contribution | Collected price per unit minus variable cash delivery/acquisition costs | State fees/taxes included; not net profit |
| Founder return per hour | Cash contribution after allocated fixed cash costs / founder hours | State allocation; not proof of sustainability |
| Channel concentration | Leads or revenue from largest channel / total for same measure | Do not mix lead share with revenue share |
| Customer concentration | Revenue from largest customer / total revenue | Same basis and window |
| Reuse benefit | Hours avoided minus creation/maintenance hours in period | Separate estimates from measurements |

Zero/unknown denominator → null / not calculable, never 0%.
Missing numerator → unknown. Numerator exceeding person-based denominator → check
cohorts/duplicates before interpretation. Show raw numerator/denominator with rates.
Compare like-for-like cohorts, channels and windows; mark immature cohorts.
Example: 40 of 100 reached people become leads, five of those 40 buy.
Capture = 40%; lead-to-purchase = 12.5%; reach-to-purchase = 5%.
These describe this cohort, not universal targets or causal proof.
With sparse data propose bounded hypotheses or measurement work, not precise claims.
