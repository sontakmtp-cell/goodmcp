# Experiment plan
Fill each field with confirmed facts, a proposed assumption or an explicit unknown.
Set thresholds before running; do not change them to make the result pass.

| Field | Required detail |
| --- | --- |
| Hypothesis | One falsifiable belief and why it matters |
| Participants | Target segment, inclusion rules and recruitment method |
| Minimum offer | Deliverable, proposed price and exclusions |
| Procedure | First actions, owner and needed drafts/tools |
| Metric | Numerator, denominator, event, time window and source |
| Sample/window | Planned eligible sample, duration, start/end and timezone |
| Success | Agreed threshold and minimum evidence |
| Failure | Result that rejects or requires changing the hypothesis |
| Inconclusive | Insufficient sample, tracking failure or conflicting evidence |
| Budget/stop | Maximum spend, founder hours, capacity and stopping trigger |
| Responsibility | Human execution/approval, AI drafting, quality checks |
| Decision | Action after success, failure or inconclusive results |

## Worked example — illustrative, not market benchmarks
Hypothesis: small studio owners will pay 300,000 VND for one manual portfolio review.
Recruit 20 eligible owners through an already authorized channel over seven days.
Offer one 30-minute review plus a one-page action list; no ongoing consulting.
Founder handles contact, payment and delivery; AI drafts the offer and action-list
template for checking. Preparing drafts does not send any contact message.

First actions: draft offer → founder checks claims/price → contact within authorized
scope → log unique eligible recipients, replies and paid bookings → deliver → review.
Launch kit includes offer draft, booking questions and delivery checklist.

Primary metric: unique eligible owners paying within the window / unique eligible
owners actually receiving the offer. Once all 20 are reached, success is at least
three paid bookings; failure is zero or one; two is inconclusive.
If fewer than 20 are reached, tracking fails or outcomes have not matured, label
inconclusive rather than substituting the planned denominator for observed reach.
Cap the test at 500,000 VND and six founder hours; stop earlier at delivery capacity.
These are example planning limits to adapt with the user, not defaults.

Record observations separately. Plan confirmation leaves the hypothesis unverified.
After results, keep/refine if supported, revise one factor if rejected, or agree a
bounded follow-up measurement if inconclusive. Preserve original thresholds.
