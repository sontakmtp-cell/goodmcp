# Opportunity evaluation: six dimensions
Score known dimensions as integers 1–5. Use 2/4 for justified intermediate cases.
Unknown = null, never 0. Identify evidence/uncertainty per score.
These are qualitative judgments, not probabilities, guarantees or market sizes.

| Key | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| pain | Minor inconvenience, easy workaround | Recurring costly problem | Urgent core task with strong evidence of costly consequences |
| leverage | Bespoke founder hours | Some reusable code/media/AI delivery | Repeatable delivery with demonstrated low incremental effort and quality controls |
| window | Crowded/closing opportunity without clear shift | Identified change, mixed timing evidence | Evidenced recent shift and accessible underserved segment |
| resource_fit | Little relevant access/capability | Some usable resources, specific gaps | Confirmed resources give direct access and credible delivery advantage |
| standardization | Every delivery redesigned | Reusable core with customization | Clear repeatable scope supported by examples/tests |
| cashflow | Long uncertain path, large upfront commitment | Plausible paid test with manageable costs | Evidence of reachable paying buyers and short feasible cash cycle |

total = sum of all six known scores, otherwise null. Founder hypotheses remain
labeled hypotheses; do not present them as measured market truth. Include
counterevidence and the cheapest next validation that could change the ranking.
Example 4, 3, null, 4, 3, 2 has total null, not 16/30 or a zero-filled score.
